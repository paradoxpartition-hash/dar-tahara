# Customer property access control

Owner: Operations/Physical Security

Approval status: draft baseline; management, operations and legal/privacy approval required before use

## Minimum rules

- Use customer-owned smart-lock or time-limited unique codes where practicable.
  A code is assigned to the smallest necessary visit/team window and revoked or
  rotated immediately after use, cancellation, staff change or suspected loss.
- Physical keys require a uniquely numbered tamper-evident tag that does not
  contain the address or customer name. Record custody on issue/return; store
  keys in a locked, access-logged cabinet separate from address mappings.
- Never place keys/codes in free-text notes, email, chat, photographs, personal
  devices or printed schedules. The approved operational system shows access
  instructions only to assigned active personnel within the required window.
- Every reveal, issue, return, failed access attempt, override, loss and
  revocation is attributable to a person and timestamp. Managers review
  exceptions daily and the access register monthly.
- Personnel verify assignment and customer-approved arrival window before
  entry. No entry after cancellation, suspension or completed service. A missed
  appointment does not authorize later access.

## Lifecycle and incidents

Operations records customer authorization, method, permitted purpose, validity,
assigned people, issue/reveal/return/revocation times and exception approvals.
Joiner/mover/leaver actions remove property-access rights before other duties are
reassigned. Inventory reconciliation occurs at least monthly and on every
custodian change.

A lost key/device, exposed code, unexplained access log, unreturned item or
entry outside the approved window is a High security event; credible
unauthorized entry or disclosure of address/access data is Critical. Notify the
incident manager and customer contact, revoke/rotate access, preserve logs,
assess property/personal-data impact, and do not conceal loss by replacing a
tag. Police/insurer/legal contact is made only by authorized roles.

Quarterly test: sample an assignment from approval through expiry, perform a
leaver revocation, reconcile keys, and simulate a lost device/code. Evidence
must show access was no longer usable and the event reached the incident path.
